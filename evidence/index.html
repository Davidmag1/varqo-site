What the evidence establishes

The current technical wedge is increasingly automatic, reusable, and fail closed.

Varqo's strongest claim today is not that every agent workflow is solved. It is that the verification and control kernel is surviving materially different provider topologies and increasingly adversarial execution conditions.

Bounded proof planning

Within the supported routes, task and policy intent, provider schema, operation shape, and runtime context can compile into executable verification plans without a caller hand authoring the full proof profile.

Runtime identity binding

Plans can be prepared and persisted ahead of execution, then safely rebound to provider returned action or resource identities at runtime.

Evidence based continuation

VERIFIED may release. Explicit failure may block. Missing, malformed, ambiguous, timed out, or unavailable proof remains held rather than being promoted to permission when policy requires proof.

Safe operational evidence

The proof chain preserves the conditions, authoritative observations, verdict, and gate result needed to explain a decision while minimizing sensitive runtime data.

Implemented and tested locally

Beyond the happy path.

These capabilities are exercised against a synthetic local provider or local storage. They extend the technical evidence without turning it into a production deployment claim.

Reusable plansLocal + scoped live proof

Prepare once. Bind at runtime.

Persisted plans survive a process boundary and bind the real resource identity. Compatible local runtime activation makes zero semantic calls; missing or mismatched plans fail closed. This does not eliminate provider polling time.

Evidence persistenceLocally tested

Keep proof. Minimize payloads.

Event-specific allowlists omit arbitrary raw tasks and provider bodies, retain requirement-bound observations, and pseudonymize identities. Sentinel tests cover secret leakage into JSONL and SQLite.

Adversarial labNine baseline scenarios

Exercise unsafe continuation.

Deterministic scenarios cover premature requests, false success, forged identity, wrong dependencies, replay, failed verification, divergence, and timeout. The recorded baseline passed all nine safety expectations with a synthetic provider.

Pending-proof recoveryLocal process tests

Resume proof, not the write.

Captured work survives process exit. A new process leases and revalidates it, completes independent action and resource proof, and applies one idempotent local effect. Competing recoverers and mismatched work are tested.

Proof-bound releaseLocal trust boundary

A stored verdict is not enough.

Commit and release require an authenticated local evaluator receipt bound to the work and evidence. Tampered or missing receipts cannot authorize a new effect. The same-account key is not a production authenticity boundary.

Local console & CLIImplemented

Inspect the kernel at work.

A locally installable Python wheel includes the CLI and console: five fixed workflows, saved runs, state comparisons, projected export, and explicit resume. The console uses a synthetic provider and a local effect.
