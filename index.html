Product north star

From permission to provable autonomous execution.

IAM answers whether an agent can act. Varqo is built to answer whether the workflow should continue now, given independently verified reality.

The long-term product is a closed integrity loop: understand what matters, verify it, control continuation, recover safely when reality diverges, and preserve evidence of every decision.

Current POC: VERIFY is the live-proven technical kernel; CONTROL and PROVE are partially implemented. Broader KNOW and generic automated RECOVER remain product direction.
